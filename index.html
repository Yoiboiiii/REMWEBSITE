Who We Are

Technical expertise for
a sustainable future

REM Synergy Sdn Bhd began as a radiological and environmental consulting company and has grown into a full-spectrum technical services provider for Malaysian industry.

Our Background

REM Synergy Sdn Bhd, formerly known as REM Consultants Sdn Bhd, was incorporated in 2017, initially as a radiological and environmental consulting company. Throughout the years, the company expanded its scope of services to regulatory compliance consultancy and on-site technical support.

Today, REM provides consultancy, training and on-site support services to various government and private companies including mining and minerals, manufacturing, oil & gas, as well as research and education institutions across Malaysia.

Services

What we offer

From radiation protection to ISO certification, our team delivers expert consulting, training, and on-site technical support.

01

Radiation Protection & Nuclear Safety and Security

Comprehensive consultancy and on-site technical support covering regulatory compliance with national and international nuclear safety standards. We assist facilities in establishing radiation protection programmes, conducting dose assessments, and ensuring physical security for nuclear and radioactive materials.

Dose AssessmentRegulatory ComplianceSecurity SystemsNORM Management
02

Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)

End-to-end EHS management services including environmental monitoring, risk assessments, and development of EHS management systems. We help organisations identify, evaluate, and control environmental and occupational health hazards in compliance with Malaysian regulations.

Environmental MonitoringRisk AssessmentEHS ManagementRegulatory Compliance
03

ISO Standards & Industrial Standards

Consultancy and training services for ISO management system standards. We guide organisations through the full implementation cycle — from gap analysis and documentation to internal audits and certification readiness — for ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 and other relevant standards.

ISO 9001ISO 14001ISO 45001Gap AnalysisInternal Audit
04

Industrial Best Practices & Sustainable Systems

Training and consultancy on sustainable system management and manufacturing best practices aligned with industry and regulatory expectations.
